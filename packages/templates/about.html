About reInsight

Transforming School Data into Better Student Outcomes

reInsight is an Education Intelligence Platform designed to help schools collect, organize and transform student data into meaningful insights that improve teaching, learning and school leadership.

Our Story

Schools generate valuable information every day—from attendance records and examination scores to behaviour reports and parent engagement. Unfortunately, much of this information remains scattered across different systems, making it difficult for educators to see the complete picture.

reInsight was created to solve this challenge by bringing all school data into one intelligent platform. Instead of simply storing information, reInsight helps schools analyse trends, identify students who may need support and make informed decisions that lead to better educational outcomes.

Our Mission

To empower schools with intelligent technology that transforms educational data into actionable insights, enabling better decisions, stronger leadership and improved student success.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading Education Intelligence Platform, helping schools around the world leverage data to create better learning experiences for every student.
